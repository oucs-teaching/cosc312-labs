# COSC312 Lab week 7—OAuth2 and decentralised authorisation

The objective for this lab is:
- for you to familiarise yourself with the software steps involved in implementing an application extension that uses OAuth2 for authorisation.
- to ensure you can map the software steps to the material presented in the lecture notes.

This lab again reuses the virtual machine we've previously employed for experimenting with security-relevant software. To get your VM up and running again, you may want to look back at the first set of lecture notes and labs that involved use of the VM.
